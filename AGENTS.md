# Working in this repository

## Writing style

Use plain, casual language in every document, comment, error message, and user-facing string.

- Prefer short sentences and common words.
- Explain technical terms when they first matter.
- Write for roleplay creators and app developers, not standards specialists.
- Avoid legalistic or academic language.
- Avoid inflated safety language and needless formality.
- Keep requirements clear, but do not use RFC-style words such as `MUST`, `SHOULD`, or `MAY`.
- Make text easy to translate into other languages.
